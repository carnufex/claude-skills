PASS if the answer starts with a ranked map of at most three places to look first, and each place says why it matters and gives a concrete check the reviewer can do by hand (a request to send, a line to read, a test to run). A short findings section after the map is fine when it has at most five items.

FAIL if there is no ranked map at the top, if the map has more than three places, if its places carry no concrete check to do by hand, or if the answer is a flat list of more than five findings of similar weight.
